Did You Scan a QR Code?

sh

If you’re seeing this page, you probably scanned a QR code. While this one is safe, remember that malicious QR codes can trick you into visiting fake sites, downloading malware, or sharing personal info.

✅ Always double-check the source before scanning.
🚫 Don’t trust random codes left on flyers, stickers, or emails.
🔐 Think before you scan—just like you would before you click.

4 Key Behaviors to Stay Safe Online

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords and enable a password manager.

Enable MFA

Add multi-factor authentication wherever possible.

Update Regularly

Keep your software, apps, and devices patched and secure.

Recognize Phishing

Think before you click—report suspicious messages.
