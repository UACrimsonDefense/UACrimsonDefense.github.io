Our General Club Meetings

Want to learn more about cyber security outside of the classroom? Come to our General Interest Meetings, where each week we will highlight a different cyber security topic related to techologies, careers, and opportunities in the field.

Past meeting topics include:

  • Ghidra Tutorial taught by NSA representatives
  • Coca-Cola Cyber Security Team Guest Speaker
  • Network Attacks using Wireshark & Aircrack-ng
  • Malware Analysis Dynamic Labs
  • Using the MITRE ATT&CK Framework for Blue Team
  • Intro to Types of Cyber Crime
  • CTF Escape Room
  • ...and more!

Interested? Stop by the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066) on Wednesday evenings at 6pm!
