October is Cyber Awareness Month! Crimson Defense will be putting QR codes up around campus to educate the dangers of scanning random QR codes.

If you scanned one of our codes, you ended up on a webpage that walks you through a three step check: point your camera instead of tapping blindly, read the actual domain instead of trusting the text around it, and only go through if the owner is who you expected.

Check it out here: [Cyber Awareness Month](/cyberawarenessmonth26).

Same rule applies everywhere, not just QR codes: emails, texts, DMs. Read the domain before you trust the link.
