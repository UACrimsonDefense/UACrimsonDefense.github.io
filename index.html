Crimson Defense: The University of Alabama's Cyber Security Club

In a world where we are becoming ever more so reliant on computers, the demand for top-tier cybersecurity professionals has never been more evident. Crimson Defense seeks to empower college students at the University of Alabama with the tools and confidence to learn critical cyber security skills before they enter the workforce. Club membership is open to any student at the University of Alabama.

Not Sure Where to Start?

Our General Meetings are a good place to start, but feel free to stop by any of our meetings! Our friendly officers will get you situated.

Project Crimson - Tuesdays at 6pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066)

Project Crimson is perfect for those who want to learn more about cyber security tools through hands-on one-on-one mentoring! Just bring your laptop and a mind ready to learn.

General Club - Wednesdays at 6pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066)

General Meetings feature introductions to cyber tools, technologies, lectures from cyber security professionals, and even interactive labs. This is our most popular day of the week!

Cert Study Group - Wednesdays at 5pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066)

Stop by if you want to elevate your cyber professional profile by studying for cyber certification exams! We specialize in CompTIA Security+ Preparation.

Competition Team - Thursdays at 6pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066)

Crimson Defense's Competition Team competes in cyber security competitions every semester. Join if you want to learn in a fast-paced environment and compete against other schools!
