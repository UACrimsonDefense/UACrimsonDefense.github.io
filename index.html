Crimson Defense: The University of Alabama's Cyber Security Club

In a world where we are becoming ever more so reliant upon computers, the need for elite cyber security professionals has become ever more apparent. Crimson Defense seeks to empower college students at the University of Alabama with the tools and confidence to learn critical cyber security skills before they enter the workforce. Club membership is open to any student at the University of Alabama.

Club Meeting Schedule

Crimson Defense General Interest meetings are every Wednesday at 6:00 pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066).

General Meetings feature introductions to cyber tools, technologies, lectures from cyber security professionals, and even interactive labs.

Our Competition Team meets every other Tuesday at 6:00 pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066).

Crimson Defense's Competition Team competes in national cyber security competitions every semester. Join if you want to learn and apply cyber skills! No experience necessary.

Project Crimson meets every Thursday at 6:00 pm in the UA Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066).

Project Crimson is perfect for those who want to learn more about cyber security tools through hands-on one-on-one mentoring! Just bring your laptop and a mind ready to learn.

Our Certification Study Group meets every Thursday at 5:30 pm in the Cyber Lab (Bevill 1066).

Stop by if you want to elevate your cyber professional profile by studying for cyber certification exams!
